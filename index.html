Worlds in my eye — While-loop

Stop-Motion Animation & Character Design 3/30/2025 – 7/15/2025

Project Description

Derived from adolescent anxiety under external praise, this animation explores inner emotional turmoil hidden beneath a minimalist exterior.

Project Brief

This work comes from a nap-dream when I was 17. During that time, teachers called me "Imagine Genius", but appreciation also brings a lot of stress to me, which has been mapped in my dream. I hope to return to the state of mind at that time by motion video, and re-examine and understand oneself again.

My world is fantastic: I would envision humans as a gel composed of code, and the power lines as the invading monsters. People often appreciate how beautiful my worlds are. Yet few have glimpsed the inner contradictions — excessive praise often leaves me feeling overwhelmed, I'm not an omnipotent, I'm just a normal teenager with some fantasy and fresh ideas. I struggle to clearly and logically articulate my perception of the world; I always feel that my language is too pale and thin which cannot exactly illustrate how worlds look in my view. It's like the explosive, vibrant colors hidden within minimalist forms.

Visual Research

Artist: JayKimEl. My color inspiration comes from the work of artist JayKimEl, whose pieces are always filled with dynamic lines and vibrant colors — very similar to the rich, vivid hues I aim to express in my own mind. That's the part I want to show but I can't in my language.

Story Outline

I stand center stage, dazzled by the spotlight. I unlock the combination lock on my head, revealing a pair of curious eyes inside. The eyes transform into a cherry bomb — the emotions I've hidden deep within me. These feelings burst forth from my mind, concealed within a Newton's cradle, the cartoon character Penelope, the sun stage backdrop, and a lollipop. Finally, a frog leaps out of my head.

I chase it past chairs, circus hoops, fraction symbols, birthday cakes, circles of life, and all kinds of imagery that symbolize a "perfect life", until I finally catch up. The frog moves unhurriedly, almost tauntingly. In an instant, my perspective shifts completely. I realize I am not standing center stage at all — I am trapped at the bottom of a Klein bottle.

Am I the frog stuck at the bottom of the bottle? Or a robot accustomed to standing center stage, yet unable to understand my own true emotions…

Fabrication & Technique

Armature and puppet fabrication: little robot guy and frog. Material tests: black paperboard, joint mobility test, two-legged nail. Storyboard and animation technique spotlight included.

Star Box

Interactive Design / Sensing Design 2025.9 – 2026.1

Sub-Title

An interactive installation exploring the concept of "Universe and Humanity".

Project Brief

This project made a motioned new media installation based on code control. Combining pulse beat and sparking light and machine movement to show the philosophy theory: universe are inherently one with the human body. Visitors are not only the work's onlooker, they are the "energy". It reveals the most fundamental core of it: activeness of universe depends on humans' activeness.

Apps Used

Code-based control, pulse/heart-rate sensing, mechanical pulleys, light and shadow space construction.

Title & Brief

Preliminary Scheme

Making Progress

Interactive Testing

Artists Inspiration

  • James Turrell; Skyspaces — made a space which lets people feel in a normal room but uses light and shadow to add levels, rebuild space, inspires reflection.
  • Moving Stillness; Bill Viola — new media artwork using water material to deconstruct the image of Mount Saint, to show the recycle of life, guide the audience to experience the passage of time and the changes of the illusions of reality.

Preliminary Scheme

PLAN 1: Projection in the space for starry sky; it interacts when people step in the area. Condition limitation: hard to create a dark space since there's no curtain in library; less interaction, hard to show the calm sense.

PLAN 2: Using sunlight reflection, cause the transparent device to rotate slowly, creating a scene of star-like light and shadow; visitors can watch them in the tent. Condition limitation: the ceiling does not allow the installation; discussion on calmness is not profound either.

Project Sketch

Materials: wire mesh, elastic fabric, star light, cotton, foamed adhesive.

Making Progress

Purchasing materials, building the structure, constructing the background, arranging the foam and elastic fabric, shaping and trimming expanding foam decorations, decorating and painting/spraying, testing interactive code, iteration adding pulleys, wiring and cable management.

Interactive Testing

Code drives steering gear plus pulleys to drive the light source and achieve mechanical movement. Heart rate sensor reads visitor's heart rate, then changes it to sparkling speed and lights' mechanical movement. Visitor's feelings drive universe's moving and sparkling — give the think of universe and human.

Storyboard

Visitor walks into installation; sits down, places finger on heart rate sensor; visitor's feelings drive universe's moving and sparkling.

An apple a day

Game Design 7/31/2025 – 9/13/2025

Concept

"Are we the part of higher level game?" This is a meta story game which combines 2D jumping and 3D space exploration. During play time, adding 2D and 3D interaction and disintegrated background. At the end, showing the philosophy thinking: ending — players just a part of higher level game.

Project Description

This project wants to show my question of this world. I am curious that does our world is the TRUE reality?

Title & Concept

Game Spec

Achievement

Test & Iteration

Final Result

Game Interactive Specification

Reference game: My shadows are bright. Use "3D blocks" in the game to build paths for the internal 2D platform levels. Design thinking: in the game, I guide players to interact with 3D objects on the table, causing changes in the 2D scene of the tabletop game, thereby achieving a visual effect that links 2D and 3D elements.

There is an incident of falling into the water. Player needs to ESC from the 2D game to 3D scene, drink (click) the water on the desk. The water in 2D game will disappear, game continued.

Achievement Collection

Interpretations of apples in the achievement. Throughout human history, apples in so many fields have been signaling to us in various ways: "Our world is a real world." But is that really true? In fact, all of these things are creations of humanity, without exception. How can we use something we ourselves created as evidence of existence?

Apples in History

  • Start — The apple core is planted, and the story begins here.
  • In physics, Newton's apple.
  • Apples in Religion — The apple of Adam and Eve.
  • Apples in Art — Rene Magritte's Apples.
  • Apple in Technology — Jobs' Apple.
  • An Apple of Discord — "For the most beautiful person".
  • END — An apple was eaten up.

Character Design & Special Collection

Character design and special collection spreads included.

Test & Iteration

Player 1 suggestion: the gameplay of switching between the interior and exterior scenes of the computer is very novel, making the entire game more philosophical and interesting. It could be improved: the exterior scenes of the computer are well done, but the platform jumping and enemy styles inside the computer are rather monotonous, and there are many minor flaws in the jumping system.

Iteration: I changed the jumping system, to avoid player stuck on wall when they jump and touch the wall. Player become more flexible and have a better playability on controlling.

MOSU

Beat Game / Vibe Coded OpenCode

MOSU

Concept

I am a beat game fan. But every time I play it there always a question wondered me: I cannot play them everywhere, also sometimes it's hard for me to touch the keys. Such as MAIMAI, the machine is too tall.

I notice that every time when I listen to music I imagined there are some taps on the road, just like the dance game. I would automatically put step on those virtual keys.

Then I inspired: How can I make this virtual taps come "true"?

Inspiration

OSU and maimai.

Process (Vibe Coded with OpenCode)

  1. Basic Finger Monitor System — TOUCHING to GET SCORE.
  2. Add Different Keys and Sound Effect — Holding Key, Tap Key, Slide Key.
  3. AI BeatMap Algorithm — achieve the beatmap generating and allow every song to be played.
  4. Testing and debug — Version 1, Version 2, Version 3.

GO HOME

3D Interactive / Memory Childhood

Concept & Process

Final Result

Concept: The Irreversible Loss of Childhood

My parents sold the old house where I had lived for ten years. Only later did I realize that along with the house, my entire childhood from age zero to sixteen was also sold. Time cannot flow backward — I can never go back.

Inspiration: Small World, Small Story

I was inspired by something include small world and record memories at that time. Such as Crystal Ball, Google Map and Music Box.

Process

  1. I drawed floor plan at first, and write tons of prompts to show the furnitures' details to AI for building a 3D model.
  2. I changed workflow: Floor Plan to Image to 3D building. As I try fix layout problem, a surprised image appeared. It inspired me: if multiple rooms cannot work well, what about concentrate all features in one room? Also as I back up my childhood memory, I realized that anime theme songs were really impressive me. So I added an interaction part: when click the poster on the wall of 3D model, songs of the anime will play.
  3. I remake it 8 times, adding more and more conditions in prompt. At the end, it looks full of my childhood memories. I focus on the anime which is the biggest and most impressive part in my childhood. I realize every time I heard the songs of them, I would have a lot of feelings on it. So I try to put music as an interactive way, when the posters are clicked it will play the songs.
  4. Then I turned it to 3D, tried different materials and structure. Then put it in OpenCode, make a first-person perspective, add the xyz coordinate axis value, then give songs as input value.

Notes

"However it doesn't look great" — iterative refinement through 8 remakes. Final turned into 3D with first-person perspective and xyz coordinates, songs as input.
